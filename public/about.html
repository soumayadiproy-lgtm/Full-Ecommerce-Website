Frequently Asked Questions
How do I pay for my order?
We accept all popular methods: UPI (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm), Debit/Credit cards, Net Banking, and Cash on Delivery (COD).
How long does delivery take?
Most orders are dispatched within 24 hours and delivered within 2 to 5 business days depending on your location.
Can I return or exchange an item?
Yes, if you received an incorrect size or damaged product, simply contact us within 7 days and we will assist you with an easy exchange or return.
How can I contact customer support?
You can message us directly on our Contact Page or call/WhatsApp us for immediate assistance.
